This Policy explains how the Operator handles personal information when you use this store, create an account, buy digital products, request programming or capstone services, or contact us. The Operator is responsible for the personal information it controls. Privacy enquiries should be sent to {{ privacy_contact_email }}.

1. Information we process

  • Account information: your name, email, password hash and, if provided, phone number and school.
  • Transactions: purchased items, amounts, payment method, transaction references, payment status, receipt images you submit, download-access records and related correspondence.
  • Service requests: project requirements, academic or organisation details you choose to provide, messages and project materials. Use anonymised examples wherever possible.
  • Security and preferences: login attempts, IP addresses and browser information received during authentication, session and anti-forgery cookies, and your locally saved theme preference.
  • Agreement records: the time of registration acceptance and the versions of the documents accepted.

Card numbers, expiry dates and security codes entered in the payment provider's hosted fields are handled by that provider. Do not send card details, passwords, government IDs or unrelated sensitive information in messages or receipt uploads. Crop or redact unnecessary balances and third-party details before uploading a receipt.

2. Purposes and lawful grounds

We process information needed to create and secure accounts, fulfil orders, check payments, deliver files, handle requested services, send operational notices and answer support requests. Contract performance and steps you ask us to take before a contract support these activities where applicable.

We may also process proportionate information to prevent fraud, investigate misuse, protect intellectual property, maintain service security and establish or defend legal claims, relying on legitimate interests where your rights do not override them. Accounting, lawful requests and other mandatory duties may require processing under a legal obligation. Where consent is the required legal ground, it must be specific to the information and purpose involved.

School and course details can reveal educational information. Supply only what is relevant to your request. We use educational or other sensitive information only for the stated purpose and with the specific consent or other legal ground required by applicable law. Registration does not authorise unrelated future use of sensitive information.

3. Your registration agreement and choices

The registration checkbox confirms that you have received, read and agreed to this Policy. Necessary account and transaction processing is described above; this agreement is not consent to advertising, sale of personal data or a waiver of privacy rights. Providing optional information is your choice. Any optional marketing or materially different consent-based processing would require a separate choice.

If you choose to provide school or academic details, the privacy checkbox also requests your explicit consent to use those details for account support and the project services you request. You may leave optional school information blank and still register. You can withdraw this consent through the privacy contact; we will explain any effect on requested services and any processing that must continue on another lawful ground.

You are responsible for accurate information and for having authority to share another person's information. Do not upload classmates' records, client databases or other people's confidential information without permission and an appropriate legal basis. Supplying a project does not transfer ownership of your personal information to us.

Access is limited to people and service providers who reasonably need it: authorised store staff and programmers handling your request, hosting/storage and email providers, payment processors, and professional advisers or authorities where necessary for a lawful purpose. We do not sell personal information or give programmers a right to use customer data for unrelated projects. Appropriate confidentiality and processing safeguards apply to providers acting on our instructions.

PayPal, GCash and any other payment provider you choose may process transaction information under their own notices and legal duties. Loading external fonts, libraries or payment components can disclose connection information, including your IP address, to those services. External providers may process information outside the Philippines; required safeguards and the Operator's applicable accountability obligations still apply.

5. Cookies, browser storage and third-party services

The site uses session and anti-forgery cookies for sign-in and security, and browser storage for the light/dark preference. Blocking necessary cookies can prevent sign-in or checkout. This Policy does not itself authorise optional advertising or tracking technologies. External links and providers have their own privacy terms.

6. Retention and security

Information is kept only as reasonably necessary for the stated purpose: account administration while the account is maintained, support and fulfilment while a request is active, and relevant records for applicable accounting, legal obligations and the establishment, exercise or defence of claims. A dispute or legal hold may justify longer retention. Records no longer needed should be deleted or anonymised; retained copies and backups remain protected.

We use measures such as password hashing, access checks and private file storage to reduce risk. No internet service can promise absolute security. This statement does not excuse our own mandatory security duties or legal responsibility. You must safeguard credentials and backups, avoid sharing accounts, and promptly report suspected unauthorised access.

7. Access, correction, objection and other rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request information about processing, access to or correction of your information, objection to processing, erasure or blocking, and portability where available. You may withdraw consent for processing that relies on it. Withdrawal does not undo earlier lawful processing; necessary processing on another valid ground and legally required records may continue. We will explain a lawful restriction or refusal rather than promise unconditional deletion of transaction or dispute records.

Send requests to {{ privacy_contact_email }}. We may verify your identity using proportionate means before releasing or changing records. You may also raise a concern with the Philippine National Privacy Commission. Nothing here removes remedies that the law provides.

Material revisions will receive a new version and appropriate notice. Where fresh consent is legally necessary, we must obtain it before the affected processing begins.
